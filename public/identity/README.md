# Sign-in artwork

These assets are used only in the corresponding PiiiN sign-in controls.

- `google-g.png`: Google-provided artwork, downloaded from https://developers.google.com/static/identity/images/g-logo.png on 2026-11-08. Guidelines: https://developers.google.com/identity/branding-guidelines
- `apple-sign-in.svg`: unchanged “Left-aligned / Black / Medium” artwork from Apple's Sign in with Apple resources, downloaded on 2026-11-08. The included `apple-license.rtf` applies. Source: https://developer.apple.com/design/resources/ . Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sign-in-with-apple

Do not recolor, crop, or distort the provider artwork.
